# Push/Pull/Legs Routine

> **Primary Goals**
>
> - Build bigger legs
> - Build a fuller chest (especially upper chest)
> - Maintain back development
> - Develop thicker, more visible abs
{: .prompt-info }

> **Training Philosophy**
>
> Prioritize **exercise quality over exercise quantity**.
>
> Progress through better execution and progressive overload, **not** by constantly adding more exercises.
{: .prompt-tip }

## Core

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Primary Function |
|-----------|-----:|------:|------------------|
| Cable Crunch | 4 | 8–12 | Loaded spinal flexion |
| Hanging Leg Raise* | 3 | 8–12 | Lower rectus / posterior pelvic tilt |

> Treat the cable crunch like any hypertrophy exercise.
>
> Progressively overload it through increased load while maintaining full spinal flexion and control.

# Progressive Overload

Use **double progression**.

1. Stay within the prescribed rep range.
2. Once every set reaches the upper rep limit with proper technique, increase the load.
3. Return to the lower end of the rep range.

Example:

| Week | Incline Press |
|------|---------------|
| 1 | 30 kg × 6,6,6,6 |
| 2 | 30 kg × 7,7,6,6 |
| 3 | 30 kg × 8,8,8,8 |
| 4 | 32.5 kg × 6,6,6,6 |

---

# Core Progression

> The abdominal muscles respond to progressive overload like every other skeletal muscle.
>
> Treat core training as hypertrophy training—not endurance training.
{: .prompt-tip }

## Cable Crunch

- 4 × 8–12
- Add repetitions until all four sets reach **12 reps**.
- Increase the weight and return to **8 reps**.

Example:

```text
50 kg × 8,8,8,8

↓

50 kg × 10,10,10,10

↓

50 kg × 12,12,12,12

↓

55 kg × 8,8,8,8
```

---

## Hanging Leg Raise

Progression:

1. Bent-knee variation
2. Straight-leg variation
3. 1-second pause at the top
4. 2–3 second eccentric
5. Dumbbell between the feet or ankle weights

> The movement finishes by **posteriorly tilting the pelvis**, not by simply raising the feet.
{: .prompt-tip }

---

## Dragon Flag

Progression:

1. Bent-leg Dragon Flag
2. Full Dragon Flag
3. Slow eccentric (3–5 seconds)
4. Weighted Dragon Flag

> Maintain a neutral lumbar position throughout the movement.
>
> Reduce the range of motion if spinal control is lost.
{: .prompt-warning }

# Cardio

- 2 × 25–35 min Zone 2
- Incline walking, cycling or rowing
- Preferably after Push/Pull sessions or on Wednesday
- Daily goal: 8,000–10,000 steps

> Cardio supports cardiovascular health, work capacity and recovery without significantly interfering with hypertrophy when performed at moderate intensity.
{: .prompt-info }

---

# Recovery

- Sleep: **7.5–9 hours/night**
- Protein: **1.6–2.2 g/kg/day**
- Small calorie surplus: **150–250 kcal/day**
- Hydrate adequately

> Recovery determines whether training produces adaptation. Poor sleep and insufficient calories reduce the quality of progressive overload.
{: .prompt-warning }

---

# Deload

Every **6–8 weeks** (or earlier if performance declines for two consecutive weeks):

- Reduce training volume by ~50%.
- Maintain technique.
- Keep moderate loads (~80–90% of normal working weights).
- Resume normal training the following week.

> Do **not** wait until pain develops before reducing fatigue.
{: .prompt-danger }

---

# Key Principles

- Prioritize **quality over quantity**.
- Substitute exercises rather than endlessly adding volume.
- Train with intent.
- Track every lift.
- Recover as seriously as you train.
